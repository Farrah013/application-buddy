# Build roadmap

## Phase 1: Evidence and scoring, estimated 4 to 6 hours

- Resume extraction and evidence inventory: 60 to 90 minutes
- Portfolio evidence placeholders: 30 minutes
- Weighted scoring engine: 90 to 120 minutes
- Tests and sample job: 60 minutes
- Documentation: 60 minutes

## Phase 2: Job intake and tracker, estimated 6 to 10 hours

- Job record schema: 60 minutes
- Requirement extraction workflow: 2 to 3 hours
- Recency validation rules: 60 to 90 minutes
- Google Sheets export: 2 to 3 hours
- Notion database export: 2 to 3 hours
- Tracker destination configuration: 30 to 60 minutes
- Review queue: 1 to 2 hours

## Phase 3: Application preparation, estimated 8 to 12 hours

- Evidence-locked resume builder: 2 to 3 hours
- Resume variant selection: 2 hours
- Evidence-bound bullet tailoring: 2 to 3 hours
- Application-question drafting: 2 hours
- Approval screen and audit log: 2 to 3 hours
- Tests: 1 to 2 hours

## Phase 4: Research and outreach, estimated 6 to 10 hours

- Public-contact verification rules: 1 hour
- People research records: 2 to 3 hours
- Email-pattern inference and verification queue: 2 to 3 hours
- Outreach generator: 2 hours
- Follow-up tracking: 1 to 2 hours
- Tests: 1 to 2 hours

## Phase 5: Submission workflow, estimated 12 to 20 hours

- Supported ATS mapping: 3 to 5 hours
- Browser workflow: 4 to 6 hours
- Sensitive-answer approval gates: 2 to 3 hours
- Submission verification: 2 to 3 hours
- Failure handling and tests: 2 to 3 hours

## Phase 6: Consulting prospecting mode, estimated 10 to 16 hours

- Define service offers and ideal client profile: 2 to 3 hours
- Prospect qualification model: 2 to 3 hours
- Separate contact and outreach schemas: 2 hours
- Public-source research workflow: 2 to 3 hours
- Outreach approval, opt-out, and follow-up rules: 2 to 3 hours
- Tests and documentation: 1 to 2 hours
