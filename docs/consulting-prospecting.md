# Consulting prospecting boundary

Application Buddy may later support outreach for AI Customer Success consulting and agent-building services. This work starts as a separate `CONSULTING_PROSPECTING` mode. A separate agent becomes preferable when service discovery, lead scoring, proposals, delivery, and client management expand beyond contact research and outreach.

The consulting mode requires its own ideal client profile, service definitions, qualification criteria, contact list, message templates, follow-up schedule, opt-out handling, and outcome tracking. Job application records never enter the consulting pipeline by default.
